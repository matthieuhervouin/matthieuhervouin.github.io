About me

 I recently completed my PhD in computer science at LAMSADE in Université Paris Dauphine under the supervision of Remzi Sanver, Olivier Cailloux and Ali Ozkes.
During my PhD, I have focused on Classification Aggregation and Proportionality for Committee Elections, and Participatory Budgeting.
I am mainly interested in computatinal social choice (COMSOC), which consists in using tools from computer science in order to solve social choice problems.
